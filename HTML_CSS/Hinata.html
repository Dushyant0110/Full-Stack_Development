Hinata Hyuga

Leader of the Hyuga Clan

About her

Hinata is a beautiful kunoichi and the former heiress of the Hyūga clan from the fictional village of Konoha. She is also a member of Team Kurenai, which consists of herself, Kiba Inuzuka with his ninja dog — Akamaru, Shino Aburame, and team leader Kurenai Yuhi.
